About the school

A supportive place to learn, question and grow

Summits International Schools is a private international school in Al-Maadi, Cairo, delivering an international curriculum in English from the earliest foundation years through to IGCSE.

Our classrooms are modern, air-conditioned and calm — places where independence, self-confidence and resilience are encouraged, and where creativity and cooperation are nurtured every day. Lessons are designed to make students investigate, think critically and apply what they learn, both in examinations and in working with others.

We aim for internationally recognised standards so that our students can access higher education anywhere in the world — while learning in a diverse community where similarities always outweigh differences.

Programmes

One school, four pathways

Whether you are looking for a gentle first classroom or internationally recognised examinations, there is a track that fits your child — and admission is open for all stages.

EYFS — Early Years

Foundation Stage · from age 2½

Learning through play in a safe, nurturing setting where our youngest students build confidence, curiosity and the first habits of school life.

American Programme

Standards-based · KG onward

A US-style pathway that values inquiry, steady individual progress and critical thinking, preparing students for universities worldwide.

British Programme

Foundation & Primary stages

A structured British-style pathway through the foundation and primary years, building strong fundamentals before the examination years.

IGCSE Division

Secondary · international exams

A dedicated IGCSE division preparing students for internationally recognised qualifications and higher education around the globe.

School life

The whole child, every day

Education at Summits goes well beyond the timetable.

Small, caring classes

With around 25 students per class, teachers know every child — their strengths, interests and the way they learn best.

Healthy, active days

A balanced diet, physical activity and time outdoors are part of the school day — keeping bodies healthy and minds ready to learn.

Global citizens

Students from many backgrounds learn together with compassion, tolerance and open-mindedness — discovering that our similarities outweigh our differences.

Ready for tomorrow

Critical thinking, communication and leadership are woven through lessons, so students leave prepared for a changing world.
